Privacy Policy — Yì Divination System

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Yì ("the app") is developed by Seth Martin. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

What Data We Collect

Force Descriptions (Casting)

When you cast a reading, the two force descriptions you enter are sent to our server for trigram mapping and hexagram interpretation. These descriptions are processed in real time and are not stored on our servers after the reading is generated.

Reading History

Your reading history is stored locally on your device only using on-device storage. We do not have access to your reading history.

No Account Required

Yì does not require an account, login, or any personal information to use.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or fingerprinting
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising data
  • No cookies

Third-Party Services

Yì uses a language model API (Moonshot / Kimi) to enhance trigram mapping and generate situational readings. Force descriptions are sent to this service for processing. No personal data is included in these requests. Moonshot's privacy policy is available at https://www.moonshot.cn/privacy-policy.

Data Retention

  • Server-side: Force descriptions are processed in memory and discarded immediately. No server-side logs of user inputs are retained.
  • Device-side: Reading history is stored locally and can be cleared at any time from within the app.

Children's Privacy

Yì does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app does not require or request personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
